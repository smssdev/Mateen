Hafiz Fahim حافظ فاهم

A Quran memorization app built on understanding: learn each verse's meaning first, link it to the verse before it and to look-alike verses, then recite it word by word while spaced repetition brings back exactly the words you stumble on.

  • Card-based recitation: recall a word, swipe to reveal, tap to mark a slip
  • 2,750+ look-alike verses detected at build time, with a warning right before the word where they part ways
  • Spaced repetition scheduled per verse from per-word strength; works offline as a PWA and an Android app
